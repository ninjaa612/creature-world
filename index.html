PUFFWING'S STORMPROOF CHALLENGE

THE BUILD BATTLE

A fort is only as good as the way it holds together. Build smart, not just big.

Build a stormproof skybase, Dummy.

Your skybase must survive a storm inspection. This is a spatial construction challenge: the inspector checks the actual positions and connections of your pieces, not just how many you placed. The 7×7 plot is labelled by row and column (for example, 3.4 means row 3, column 4).

  1. Foundation: make exactly nine floor tiles in one complete, connected 3×3 square. You may place it anywhere on the plot; a central position leaves room for the ramp.
  2. Walls and entrance: the eight outer squares of that 3×3 foundation must contain exactly seven walls and one door. The centre square must remain an open floor. The door must replace one outer wall; it cannot be placed outside the foundation.
  3. Roof: add a roof piece to every one of the nine foundation squares, including the centre. A roof floating outside the foundation will fail inspection.
  4. Access ramp: place exactly one ramp on a grass square directly beside the door (up, down, left or right — diagonal contact does not count). The ramp must be outside the foundation and point towards the doorway.
  5. Material budget: you start with 32 wood and 18 stone. The required build costs 29 wood and 17 stone: floors 9W; seven walls 7W/7S; door 2W/1S; roof 9W/9S; ramp 2W. You have only a small spare supply, so plan your placements.
  6. Tools: choose a tool, then tap a plot square. Tap the same placed piece again to remove just that piece. The Eraser clears every piece on that square and refunds the materials. Walls, doors and roofs need a floor underneath.
  7. Inspection: press CHECK STRUCTURE whenever you like. A failed check costs no materials or lives, but the storm clock keeps running. The inspector tells you what is wrong. You must fix it before time reaches zero.
  8. Storm: you have 2 minutes 15 seconds. The clock starts only after Start and a three-second countdown. There is no pause and no multiple-choice shortcut.

Before you enter the arena…

This is a turn-based duel, not a reflex test. There is no timer. You choose one action, Vexori responds, and then your next turn begins. You must defeat both of Vexori's forms in the same battle.

⚡ QUICK STRIKEDeals 15–23 damage depending on your fighter and restores 20 energy. Use it to build energy.
✨ SIGNATURE MOVECosts 40 energy and deals heavy damage. Each creature's signature has its own special effect.
🛡️ GUARDRestores 24 energy and reduces the next incoming attack by 65%. A guard stays ready if Vexori only charges and does not attack yet.
💗 RECOVEROne use per battle. Restores up to 30 HP. Save it for a dangerous moment, not the first scratch.

Read Vexori's tells
