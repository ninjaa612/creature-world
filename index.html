Three laps. Two rivals. One very overconfident fire gremlin. Show Blazbit you can actually drive.

🏎️ Race briefing

↑ / WAccelerate — build speed on straights.
↓ / SBrake — slow down before tight corners.
← →Steer — change lanes to pass rivals. A/D also work.
SPACEBoost — spend limited energy on straights and overtakes.
QEnter pit lane — approach the blue PIT ENTRY marker, stay near the centre line and press Q.
PPit stop — follow the blue lane to BOX 07, brake below 35 km/h, choose tyres and press P. Service takes 3.4 seconds.
1 / 2 / 3Tyres — select Soft, Medium or Hard with 1/2/3 before starting your service.

🏆 Your objectives

• Complete 3 laps before the 55-second timer ends.
• Finish in 1st or 2nd place.
• Complete a pit stop in BOX 07.
• Avoid track-limit penalties and preserve your tyres.

🛞 Choose your starting tyres

Tip: Soft is quick but wears faster. Hard lasts longer but turns less sharply.

PIT PROCEDURE: ① Reach the blue PIT ENTRY marker → ② press Q → ③ follow the blue lane → ④ brake below 35 km/h at BOX 07 → ⑤ choose tyres (1/2/3) → ⑥ press P and wait 3.4 seconds → ⑦ drive through PIT EXIT. The lap counter must keep its place throughout.

The clock stays OFF until you press Start. A three-second light sequence follows. The 55-second race timer starts on the green light.
